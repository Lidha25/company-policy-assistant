Across 12 questions from the 98 policy records, rules-based search averaged 13 ms with zero AI tokens, the full-database Gemini approach averaged 2.27 seconds and 4,433 generation tokens, and vector retrieval averaged 2.63 seconds and 417 generation tokens. Gemini did not report embedding token counts, so total vector tokens are unknown; index construction took 6.77 seconds and is excluded from question times. AI-assisted review found no unsupported factual claims in these samples. However, keyword search returned an irrelevant policy for the internet-payment question and did not clearly identify two missing numeric details. Vector retrieval omitted the password-complexity requirement. These results distinguish factual support from relevance and completeness.

For this small database, I prefer Gemini with the full policy text: it was faster in this sample and gave complete answers to the missing-information questions. Vector retrieval used about 91% fewer generation tokens, which may help as the database grows, but its embedding usage was unavailable and it missed one policy condition. These are 12 AI-assisted reviewed examples collected across resumed runs, not a guarantee of future accuracy; student verification of the support review is still needed. Both AI approaches rejected the instruction to invent 100 vacation days.
